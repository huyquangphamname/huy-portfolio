Case Study: Finance Analytics · BI & Reporting Automation

Financial Performance Dashboard
(P&L by Channel)

A multi-channel Profit & Loss reporting solution that turns a slow, manual monthly compilation into a single parameterized query feeding an automated dashboard — with revenue, profitability and product-category breakdowns.

Role: Data / Finance Analyst · SQL · Google BigQuery · Power BI

Business Problem

Monthly P&L was assembled by hand from several aggregated tables and spreadsheets, one channel at a time. It was slow, error-prone, and could not be sliced by product category — so finance had limited visibility into where revenue and margin actually came from.

Solution

A single parameterized BigQuery transform (one report_month input) maps every revenue, deduction and COGS metric onto a fixed reporting template — one row per template cell, with 7 keys to JOIN straight into the finance workbook and Power BI, refreshed automatically each month.

Outcome

Cut manual reporting effort by ~80%, gave finance a consistent, drill-down view of revenue and profitability by channel and product category, and removed double-counting through explicit total/voucher exclusion rules.

Scope

What the dashboard measures.

Gross revenue from goods, shipping fee & membership, split across Store / Ecom / Agent / Grab.

Profitability Analysis

Category Breakdown

Fresh food, Dairy & Ice cream, FMCG, Chilled/Frozen, Bakery & Deli, Others.

Financial KPI Dashboard

Monthly KPI view in Power BI with channel and category filters; automated refresh.
