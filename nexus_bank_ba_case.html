Project Journey

From a complex, drop-off-heavy banking signup to a unified, personalized finance app.

Friction & fragmentation in mobile banking

  • •Complex multi-step signup → high new-user drop-off.
  • •Apps limited to fixed services; no holistic money management.
  • •Crude risk assessment; little personalization.

Unified, personalized finance platform

MVP epics

  • • Express onboarding: phone/OTP + eKYC (OCR CCCD + face match)
  • • Login via PIN/biometrics, new-device verification
  • • QR transfer (static/dynamic) + transaction history
  • • PFM: auto-categorize, budgets, spending reports & alerts

Non-functional & security

  • • TLS 1.3 / AES-256, MFA for high-value transfers
  • • ≤3s QR transfer, 10k concurrent users / 100 TPS
  • • Uptime 99.9%, RPO=0, RTO ≤15m
  • • Personas: Finance / Investor / ESG (Phase 2)
